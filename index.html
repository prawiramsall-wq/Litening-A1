Listening Exercise

Listen to the audio carefully and answer the questions.

1. What is the speaker talking about?




2. When does the class take place?




3. What time does the class start?




4. Where is the class held?




5. How much does the course cost?




6. How many classes are included?




7. How long is each class?




8. What should participants bring?




9. Who provides the yoga mats?




10. How can people join?
